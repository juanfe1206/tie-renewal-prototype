# Team roles and contributions

Service: renewal of the residence permit and TIE card, Madrid (Oficina de Extranjería + Policía Nacional).

| Team member | Half | Individual contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Manuela Calle** | Citizen (demand side) | **Research:** mapped today's renewal journey and every touchpoint with the immigration office and the police (filing window, 3-month decision and silence rule, the fingerprint-appointment shortage, the second trip to collect the card), using RD 1155/2024 and Ministry fact sheets.<br>**Citizen types and use cases:** 6 personas (employee, retiree filing late, family reunification, card-only renewal, vulnerable user, citizen using an AI assistant) and 10 use cases, including corrections, status checks, refusal and appeal, complaints and abuse (bots, duplicate filings, forged documents).<br>**Service contract:** negotiated v1.1 with the administration half. Set the scope (renewal only, Madrid) and added three citizen conditions: the paused clock is always visible, nothing is revoked without warning, and plain language plus an assisted channel are available at every step. Corrected the family-reunification silence rule after checking the source.<br>**Prototype, citizen side:** reminder, sign-in or assisted help, eligibility check, pre-filled application with "this is wrong" corrections, fee, confirmation, status tracker with the 90-day clock, fix-it loop, answering an officer's question, auto-assigned appointment (change once), card ready, refusal and appeal, messages, help and rights. Includes assisted mode, 4 languages with Arabic right-to-left, and a duplicate-application guard.<br>**Shared foundations:** design system (tokens, components, documentation page), shared case model and demo engine (`js/data.js`) that the officer console runs on, synthetic personas and ~50 background cases, and the README integration contract for the officer side.<br>**Citizen communication:** drafts of the citizen guide / brochure (`Citizen guide - TIE renewal (long version).html`). |
| **Juan Felipe** | Administration (service side) | _To complete._ From the repo: officer console (`officer/`) with case queue, case detail, flag review, rules and auto-grant log, abuse and fraud watch, sample and override audit, dashboard with fairness by channel and language, and an "how AI is used here" page, built on the shared engine and design system. Co-negotiated service contract v1.1 (admin side: data sources, automation rules, targets, red-flag review, appeals). |
