Design system

Tokens and components for the residence and TIE renewal service. The citizen app and the officer app both use these files, so the two sides look and behave as one service.

Principles

  1. Plain and trustworthy. Square edges, one brand colour, no decoration. It should look like a public service, not a marketing site.
  2. Status is always visible. Every screen tells the person where their case is and what happens next.
  3. Accessible by default. WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, a yellow focus ring on everything, 28px touch targets, RTL support for Arabic, and an assisted mode with larger type.
  4. Semantic colour only. Green means done or go, amber means action needed, red means problem. Colour never carries meaning alone: there's always a word too.
  5. Tokens, never literals. Components read every colour and size from tokens.css.
