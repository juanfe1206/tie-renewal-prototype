Renew your residence and TIE in Madrid

Renew your residence and TIE in days, not months

One online request. One police visit. Live status.

People often call this "renewing the NIE". Your NIE number never expires. What you renew is your residence permit and your TIE card.

Hi, I am Tico. I will walk you through it.

Who it is for

Made for non-EU residents in Madrid

Workers

Your residence and work permit is expiring.

Retirees

You hold a non-lucrative permit.

Families

You hold a family-reunification permit.

Anyone who needs help

Limited Spanish, a disability, or no digital ID.

Before and after

Six steps, each one made easier

Today New service
  1. 1
    Today

    You remember the deadline yourself

    Nobody tells you when your permit is about to expire.

    New

    An automatic reminder

    You get a message about 90 days before expiry.

  2. 2
    Today

    You re-upload papers they already have

    You file the renewal with Extranjería and send the same documents again.

    New

    One pre-filled online request

    It uses data the administration already holds. You confirm "nothing changed" and pay the fee online.

  3. 3
    Today

    You wait with no clear status

    In Madrid the wait was long.

    88 daysaverage wait
    65%decided within 90 days

    New

    A live status tracker

    Every update is sent to you. Clear-cut renewals are decided within 5 working days (target).

    1. Request received
    2. Checking your data
    3. Decision
    4. Fingerprint appointment
    5. Card ready

  4. 4
    Today

    You hunt for a "cita de huellas"

    In Madrid a police fingerprint appointment takes weeks to months to find. Bots and resellers grab the slots.

    New

    Your appointment is assigned for you

    It comes with the decision, within 10 working days. You can reschedule it once. No hunting, no bots.

  5. 5
    Today

    The fingerprint appointment

    You go to the police for your fingerprints.

    New

    You go once, for your fingerprints.

    Today

    You go back 1 to 2 months later.

    New

How to start

Three steps to file your renewal

1

Get the reminder

It comes by SMS, email or letter. Or go to the service page.

2

Sign in

Use Cl@ve or a digital certificate. No digital ID? Ask for assisted or in-person help.

3

Confirm, pay and submit

  • Confirm your data.
  • Say whether anything changed.
  • Pay the fee online: Modelo 790-012, about €16–22.
  • Submit.

Your rights and how AI is used

You stay in control of your data

Data from other administrations

The service pulls your data from other administrations under Ley 39/2015, art. 28.

You can object.

Automation approves clear-cut cases only

Officers then spot-check a random sample of those approvals.

If a check finds a problem

You are told and you can respond.

Nothing is revoked silently

You always hear about it first.
