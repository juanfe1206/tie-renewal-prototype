How it works

From reminder to card in five steps

  1. 1

    90 days before expiry

    You get a reminder

    By SMS, email or letter, with your reference number.

  2. 2

    From 2 months before · 10 minutes

    You apply online

    Sign in with Cl@ve or a digital certificate. Check the details we already have, say if anything changed, and pay the fee online.

  3. 3

    5 working days · 30 if an officer checks

    We check and decide

    If everything matches, you're approved automatically. If something changed, an officer looks at it.

  4. 4

    Within 10 working days of the decision

    Your appointment arrives

    We book your fingerprint appointment for you. You can change it once online.

  5. 5

    Within 15 days of fingerprints

    Your TIE card is ready

    Valid for 4 years.

What you need

  • Cl@ve or a digital certificate, or help from a person
  • A bank card for the fee (Modelo 790, code 012)
  • Nothing to upload in most cases

While you wait, your current permit stays valid, even if it expires before we decide.

Something missing

We tell you exactly what's wrong and how to fix it, often in one click. You get 10 days. Your application is never closed automatically.

A question from an officer

If something doesn't match, an officer shows you what they found and the evidence. You get 10 to 15 days to answer, in any language. Nothing is refused before you answer.

If you disagree

Ask for a review (recurso de reposición) within 1 month. A different official decides, and you can do it online. Or go to court within 2 months.
